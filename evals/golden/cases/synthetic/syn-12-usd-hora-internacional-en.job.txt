Senior Backend Engineer (Node.js)

Acme Cloud Labs | Remote | Location: United States | Independent contractor

About us
Acme Cloud Labs builds scheduling and billing software for physical therapy clinics across the United States. We are a distributed team of 25 engineers and we ship small changes to production every day.

What you will do
- Design and build APIs for appointment booking, insurance claims and payments;
- Own services end to end, from design documents to monitoring in production;
- Improve the performance of our PostgreSQL queries and background jobs;
- Review pull requests and mentor other engineers;
- Join planning sessions with product managers and designers.

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional backend experience;
- Strong Node.js and TypeScript skills;
- PostgreSQL;
- AWS (ECS, RDS, SQS);
- Docker;
- Fluent English: all meetings, documents and code reviews are in English.

Nice to have
- Kubernetes;
- GraphQL;
- Experience with healthcare or insurance data.

Engagement and compensation
- Full-time independent contractor engagement (40 hours per week);
- USD 35-45 per hour, depending on experience, paid monthly;
- At least 4 hours of overlap with US Eastern Time.

Perks
- Flexible schedule outside the overlap window;
- Annual learning stipend for courses and conferences;
- One-time home office equipment stipend.

Hiring process
Short call with our recruiting team, a 90-minute live technical interview, a system design conversation and a final chat with the engineering manager.
